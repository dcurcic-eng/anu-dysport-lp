SAFETY & SUITABILITY

What to know before you book.

Dysport is an FDA-approved botulinum toxin type A. Amy reviews your history before she treats you.

Good candidates

Adults 18 and over seeking non-surgical wrinkle reduction, with realistic expectations.

Common side effects

Temporary redness, mild swelling or bruising, usually resolving within 24 to 48 hours.

Rare side effects

Temporary muscle weakness, headaches, or allergic reactions.

Not suitable

Pregnancy or breastfeeding, neuromuscular disorders, aminoglycoside antibiotics or blood thinners, or an active skin infection at the site.

Aftercare

Stay upright for four hours, don't rub the treated areas, postpone strenuous exercise for 24 hours, and return at two weeks.
